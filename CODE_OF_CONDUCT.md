# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be kind. Assume good faith. Remember that the person you are replying to is
probably very tired.

## Why this matters here

This is a project about babies, built largely by people in the middle of caring for
one. Contributors and users show up sleep-deprived, sometimes worried about their
child's health, often asking for help with something that frightens them. That
raises the bar for how we treat each other.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers and with people whose first
  language is not English.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. "This breaks on midnight crossings"
  is useful; "did you even test this" is not.
- Accept that people parent differently. Breastfeeding or formula, co-sleeping or
  cot, schedules or on-demand — none of these are debates this project takes a
  side in, and none belong in code review.
- Respect privacy. Never ask someone to share data about their child, and never
  post someone else's.
- Assume the other person knows something you do not.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or jokes, including about parenting choices, family
  structure, disability, or a child's health or development
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind
- Publishing anyone's private information, including photos of or details about
  their children
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or persistently reopening settled decisions
- Giving medical advice to someone who came here with a worried question. Point
  them to a doctor instead — kindly, and without diagnosing anything.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit
messages, code comments — and to anyone representing the project publicly.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the
maintainers by opening a
[security advisory](https://github.com/beingmechon/baby-tracker/security/advisories/new),
which is the private reporting channel available on this repository. Public issues
are not the place for a report.

Include what happened, where, and links if you have them. You do not need to have
a complete account, and you will not be asked to confront the person involved.

Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers who are the subject of a report
are recused from handling it.

## Enforcement

Depending on severity and pattern, maintainers may:

1. Give a private warning explaining what was wrong and what is expected instead.
2. Ask for a public apology or the removal of a comment.
3. Temporarily block someone from participating.
4. Permanently ban someone from the project.

Serious violations — harassment, threats, or posting private information about a
child — go straight to a permanent ban.

Maintainers who do not uphold this document in good faith may themselves be removed
from the project.

## In short

We are trying to make a hard part of life a bit easier. Act like it.
