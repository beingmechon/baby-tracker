Feeds, sleep and diapers in one tap — designed to be used one-handed, in the dark,
while holding a baby. Everything is stored on your own device. There is no server
to sign up to, because there is no server.

Free forever, including the parts other trackers paywall:

* Feeding — a nursing stopwatch with per-side timing that remembers which side was
  last used, and bottle logging in ml or oz.
* Sleep — one big start/stop timer, classified as a nap or night sleep from your
  own night hours, and it survives closing the app.
* Sleep patterns — the next nap predicted from this baby's own wake windows, a
  24-hour day wheel, a week of nights and naps, and the trend against last week.
  All computed on the device.
* Growth with real WHO percentile charts — weight, length and head circumference
  against the World Health Organization's own reference curves.
* Reminders — next feed, diaper, pumping or anything you name, handed to Android
  so they arrive whether the app is open or closed.
* Pumping and a milk stash, listed in the order to use it.
* Temperature and medication, compared against what health services publish and
  never diagnosed.
* A symptom diary and doctor visits, with the questions to ask, printable for an
  appointment.
* Handover — what to tell whoever has the baby next, as a message you can copy.
* More than one baby, each with their own log.
* Your data is yours: JSON backup, CSV export for the paediatrician, and one-tap
  deletion of everything.

Privacy is the point, not a feature. The app makes no network requests at all
after it is installed — not analytics, not crash reporting, not an ad network.
There is no account and nothing to breach. Because everything is local, your data
is only as safe as your device, so export a backup now and then.

Not a medical device. It records what you tell it and shows you your own numbers
back. It does not diagnose anything and it is not a substitute for your
paediatrician.
